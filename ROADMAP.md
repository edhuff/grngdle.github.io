# gRNGdle development roadmap

The prototype implements Classical integers, expanded integer bands, and ordered sums and products. The remaining notation families are planned.

| Cumulative milestone | Active play target | Delivery |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First integer expansion | 8–12 h | Playable. Extend the Classical region to 80–120 total studies, with 30–50 on the main route and optional collection and efficiency branches. |
| Advanced arithmetic | 20–60 h | Sums, products, and nesting are playable. Add more arithmetic grammars and exact domain checks. |
| Exponentiation and factorials | 60–120 h | Add integer powers and factorials as typed operators with controlled operands, lazy formatting, and bounded exact evaluation. |
| Power towers and tetration | 120–250 h | Add right-associated towers and integer tetration. Document height conventions and certified comparison rules. |
| Higher hyperoperations | 250–500 h | Add generalized integer ranks and Knuth up-arrows, with rank and arrow-count badges and explicit domains. |
| Conway chained arrows | 500–900 h | Add the established chain grammar and recurrence rules. Keep rendering and inspection bounded. |
| Ordinal-indexed functions | 900–1,500 h | Pick a specific ordinal notation and fundamental-sequence convention. Add a corresponding fast-growing hierarchy and domain validation. |
| Extreme googology | 1,500–2,500+ h | Add formally specified Busy Beaver variants as symbolic expressions. Only show values or comparisons where a proof or trusted bound is available. |

## Scale the research network

Build toward roughly 1,100 studies in nine disciplines. Give every era an entry breakthrough, several optional economic and discovery branches, and exits that join existing disciplines. Add studies in data modules grouped by era, with stable node IDs, graph coordinates, prereq rules, and reusable effect types. Validate IDs, dependencies, reachability, costs, and layout in CI. Retain earlier generator choices in each era and version changes to their distributions.

As the map grows, add discipline and era search, jump links, lane collapsing, and viewport culling while keeping direct prerequisite edges visible. Give each major unlock an inspection view for its exact sampling rules and mathematical domains.

## Validate pacing and collection quality

Simulate alternative purchase routes and specialization choices, then compare them with observed play cadence. Tune minor purchases and milestones separately. Keep all EP tied to individual player actions and track active hours as estimates derived from manual rolls. Extend records with generator-version-aware probability fixtures, structural badges, and safe certified comparisons. Benchmark archive and map performance and migrate saves with tested fixtures whenever the AST, probability, or research schema changes.
